ABOUT THE LIBRARY

The Eco Materials Library is a hands-on, self-guided resource for learning about and experimenting with eco materials located in the ATLAS Institute at the University of Colorado Boulder.

It connects a physical collection of material samples with recipes and digital documentation, ingredients, tools, and equipment, giving people a pathway from encountering a material to understanding how it is made and trying it themselves.

Visitors can browse the collection, choose a sample, open its documentation, and use the recipe as a starting point for making.

DESIGN STRATEGY

COLOR

To design a comprehensive branding identity, the initiative looked at the categories of material samples in the library and used a color palette to identify the eight different categories showcased.

The categories are classifications based on the material's primary composition and source.

The color palette was applied across the library as a coding mechanism:

  • Logo for hanging tags
  • Library shelves
  • Sample documentation on the website

STYLE GUIDE

LABELS

The library used two different sized labels to showcase each sample, giving it a name, a base decided by what its primary ingredient is, a code for where it is in the library, ingredients, instructions, post-making instructions, and credits and alterations.

For the design, the labels had to look the same but adapt to the different sizes and still be readable. They also had to have adjusted layouts based on the number of icons needed in each section, with some labels having only two icons and others having three or four.

Through iteration, the design incorporated a numbering system between the sections to help visually guide a reader through the information when used for making a sample in the lab.
